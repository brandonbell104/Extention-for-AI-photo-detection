Known Invisible AI Watermarks

SynthID by Google DeepMind No public detector
Embedded in images from Google Imagen, Gemini, and Veo. Imperceptible pixel-level watermark that survives cropping, compression, and filters. Only Google can verify it internally.
Content Credentials (C2PA) by Adobe / CAI Coalition Public verifier
Metadata-based provenance standard used by Adobe Firefly, OpenAI DALL-E 3, Microsoft Designer, Leica cameras, and others. Verifiable at contentcredentials.org/verify. Not a pixel watermark -- it's cryptographically signed metadata that can be stripped.
Stable Signature by Meta No public detector
Invisible watermark trained into the Stable Diffusion decoder. Survives JPEG compression, cropping, and brightness changes. No public verification tool available.
DALL-E Metadata by OpenAI C2PA verifiable
DALL-E 3 and ChatGPT images include C2PA Content Credentials. Also adds a visible colored stripe (which can be cropped). Verify via C2PA checker above.
PhotoDNA by Microsoft Law enforcement only
Perceptual hash-based system primarily used for CSAM detection. Not an AI watermark, but used for image identification. Not publicly accessible.
Digimarc by Digimarc Corp Enterprise only
Commercial invisible watermarking used by stock photo agencies and publishers. Survives print and scan. Enterprise licensing required for detection.
