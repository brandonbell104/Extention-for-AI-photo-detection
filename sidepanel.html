Watermark Database
AI image generators may embed invisible watermarks in their output. Most have no public verification tool. The only widely verifiable standard is C2PA Content Credentials.
SynthID Google DeepMind No public detector
Pixel-level watermark in Imagen, Gemini, and Veo outputs. Survives cropping, compression, and filters. Only Google can verify internally.
Content Credentials Adobe / CAI Public verifier
Crypto-signed metadata used by Firefly, DALL-E 3, Designer, and Leica cameras. Verify at contentcredentials.org. Can be stripped from the file.
Stable Signature Meta No public detector
Invisible watermark baked into Stable Diffusion decoder. Survives JPEG compression, cropping, and brightness changes.
DALL-E Metadata OpenAI C2PA verifiable
DALL-E 3 and ChatGPT images include C2PA Content Credentials. Also adds a visible colored stripe that can be cropped.
PhotoDNA Microsoft Restricted
Perceptual hash system for image identification. Law enforcement only. Not an AI watermark.
Digimarc Digimarc Corp Enterprise
Commercial invisible watermarking for stock agencies. Survives print and scan. Enterprise licensing required.
